# Performance Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes a comprehensive framework for managing employee performance, ensuring alignment with organizational goals, and supporting professional development.

## Performance Management Cycle

### Annual Performance Reviews
- Conducted between January and March each year
- All employees participate regardless of tenure
- 360-degree feedback incorporated for management roles
- Performance ratings directly impact compensation decisions

### Mid-Year Check-ins
- Mandatory sessions held in July
- Progress assessment against annual goals
- Course correction opportunities
- Development plan updates

### Quarterly One-on-Ones
- Monthly meetings between employee and direct supervisor
- Informal feedback and coaching
- Project updates and obstacle identification
- Career development discussions

## Goal Setting Framework

### SMART Goals
All performance goals must be:
- **Specific**: Clearly defined outcomes
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable metrics
- **Achievable**: Realistic given resources
- **Relevant**: Aligned with business objectives
- **Time-bound**: Clear deadlines established

### Goal Categories
**Business Goals (60%)**
- Revenue targets
- Project deliverables
- Customer satisfaction metrics
- Quality standards

**Development Goals (25%)**
- Skill acquisition
- Training completion
- Certification achievements
- Leadership development

**Behavioral Goals (15%)**
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Communication effectiveness
- Innovation and creativity
- Cultural alignment

## Performance Rating Scale

### Rating Levels
**5 - Exceptional (5%)**
- Consistently exceeds all expectations
- Demonstrates exceptional leadership
- Drives significant business impact
- Serves as role model for others

**4 - Exceeds Expectations (20%)**
- Regularly surpasses performance targets
- Takes initiative beyond assigned duties
- Contributes to team and organizational success
- Demonstrates strong potential for advancement

**3 - Meets Expectations (60%)**
- Achieves all performance objectives
- Fulfills job requirements effectively
- Contributes positively to team goals
- Shows steady professional growth

**2 - Below Expectations (10%)**
- Fails to meet some performance standards
- Requires additional support and guidance
- Shows potential for improvement
- May need performance improvement plan

**1 - Unsatisfactory (5%)**
- Consistently fails to meet expectations
- Performance significantly impacts team/business
- Immediate intervention required
- May result in termination if not improved

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

### Initiation Criteria
- Performance rating of 2 or below
- Significant performance decline
- Failure to meet critical objectives
- Behavioral issues affecting work quality

### PIP Process
**Week 1-2: Plan Development**
- Manager and HR collaborate on plan
- Specific improvement areas identified
- Clear expectations and timelines set
- Resources and support defined

**Week 3-10: Implementation**
- Weekly check-ins with supervisor
- Progress monitoring and documentation
- Additional training or coaching provided
- Regular feedback and adjustment

**Week 11-12: Final Evaluation**
- Comprehensive performance assessment
- Decision on plan success or failure
- Next steps determined (continuation, promotion, or termination)

### Success Metrics
- Specific performance targets met
- Behavioral improvements demonstrated
- Consistent progress throughout period
- Positive feedback from colleagues and customers

## Career Development

### Individual Development Plans (IDP)
- Created during annual review process
- Aligned with career aspirations and business needs
- Updated quarterly based on progress
- Includes stretch assignments and learning opportunities

### Promotion Criteria
**Internal Promotion Requirements**
- Minimum 18 months in current role
- Consistent "Meets" or "Exceeds" performance ratings
- Demonstrated readiness for next level responsibilities
- Completion of required training and certifications

**Leadership Track**
- Management training completion
- Mentoring experience
- Cross-functional project leadership
- Strategic thinking demonstration

### Succession Planning
- High-potential employee identification
- Leadership pipeline development
- Cross-training and knowledge transfer
- Emergency succession protocols

## Feedback and Recognition

### Continuous Feedback Culture
- Real-time feedback encouraged
- Both positive recognition and constructive criticism
- Peer-to-peer feedback systems
- Customer feedback integration

### Recognition Programs
**Spot Recognition**
- Immediate acknowledgment of exceptional work
- Monetary rewards up to $500
- Public recognition in team meetings
- Digital badges and certificates

**Annual Awards**
- Employee of the Year
- Innovation Award
- Customer Service Excellence
- Team Achievement Award

**Long-term Recognition**
- Service anniversary celebrations
- Professional development opportunities
- Conference attendance and speaking
- Special project assignments

## Performance Documentation

### Required Documentation
- Goal setting worksheets
- Regular one-on-one meeting notes
- Performance review forms
- Development plan updates
- Recognition and disciplinary actions

### Documentation Standards
- Objective, factual language
- Specific examples and metrics
- Timely completion and filing
- Confidential handling and storage

## Manager Responsibilities

### Performance Management Duties
- Set clear expectations and goals
- Provide regular feedback and coaching
- Document performance consistently
- Support employee development
- Address performance issues promptly

### Training Requirements
- New manager orientation
- Performance management certification
- Annual refresher training
- Bias awareness and mitigation

## Employee Rights and Responsibilities

### Employee Rights
- Fair and objective performance evaluation
- Regular feedback and communication
- Access to development opportunities
- Appeal process for disputed ratings
- Confidential handling of performance data

### Employee Responsibilities
- Active participation in performance process
- Honest self-assessment and reflection
- Commitment to improvement and development
- Professional behavior and attitude
- Timely completion of required activities
