# Recruitment and Hiring Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes standardized procedures for recruiting, interviewing, and hiring qualified candidates while ensuring compliance with employment laws and promoting diversity and inclusion.

## Equal Employment Opportunity

### Non-Discrimination Commitment
We are committed to equal employment opportunity and prohibit discrimination based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, or national origin
- Religion or creed
- Sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Age (40 and older)
- Disability or medical condition
- Veteran or military status
- Genetic information
- Any other protected characteristic

### Affirmative Action
- Proactive outreach to underrepresented groups
- Diverse candidate slate requirements
- Inclusive interview panel composition
- Regular diversity metrics monitoring and reporting

## Recruitment Process

### Job Requisition
**Approval Requirements**
- Department head authorization
- Budget approval confirmation
- HR review and compliance check
- Final approval from executive leadership

**Position Documentation**
- Detailed job description
- Required qualifications and skills
- Compensation range and benefits
- Reporting structure and team dynamics

### Sourcing Strategies
**Internal Recruitment**
- Internal job postings for 5 business days
- Employee referral program incentives
- Internal mobility and transfer opportunities
- Succession planning candidate consideration

**External Recruitment**
- Professional job boards and career sites
- University partnerships and campus recruiting
- Professional associations and industry networks
- Social media and digital marketing campaigns
- Executive search firms for senior positions

### Candidate Screening

**Application Review**
- Minimum qualification verification
- Resume and cover letter assessment
- Portfolio or work sample evaluation
- Reference and background check initiation

**Initial Screening**
- Phone or video screening interview
- Basic qualification confirmation
- Cultural fit preliminary assessment
- Salary expectation alignment

## Interview Process

### Interview Panel Composition
- Hiring manager (required)
- HR representative (required)
- Peer-level team member
- Cross-functional stakeholder
- Senior leadership (for management roles)

### Interview Structure
**First Round: Technical Assessment**
- Role-specific skill evaluation
- Problem-solving scenarios
- Technical knowledge verification
- Work sample or presentation review

**Second Round: Behavioral Interview**
- Competency-based questioning
- Cultural fit assessment
- Leadership and teamwork evaluation
- Career motivation and goals discussion

**Final Round: Executive Interview**
- Strategic thinking assessment
- Long-term vision alignment
- Compensation negotiation
- Final decision and offer preparation

### Interview Guidelines
**Preparation Requirements**
- Review candidate materials thoroughly
- Prepare structured interview questions
- Understand legal interview boundaries
- Coordinate with other panel members

**Prohibited Questions**
- Personal relationships or marital status
- Pregnancy or family planning
- Religious beliefs or practices
- Political affiliations or opinions
- Financial status or credit history
- Disability or health conditions (unless job-related)

## Selection and Decision Making

### Evaluation Criteria
**Technical Competency (40%)**
- Required skills and experience
- Industry knowledge and expertise
- Problem-solving abilities
- Innovation and creativity

**Cultural Fit (30%)**
- Alignment with company values
- Communication and collaboration style
- Adaptability and learning agility
- Professional demeanor and attitude

**Leadership Potential (20%)**
- Decision-making capabilities
- Influence and persuasion skills
- Strategic thinking ability
- Team building and mentoring

**Growth Potential (10%)**
- Career ambition and motivation
- Continuous learning commitment
- Scalability with company growth
- Long-term retention likelihood

### Decision Process
**Consensus Building**
- Individual interviewer feedback collection
- Panel discussion and debate
- Objective criteria evaluation
- Final recommendation development

**Approval Hierarchy**
- Hiring manager recommendation
- HR compliance review
- Department head approval
- Executive sign-off (for senior roles)

## Background Checks and Verification

### Standard Verification
**Employment History**
- Previous employer confirmation
- Job title and responsibility verification
- Employment dates and reason for leaving
- Eligibility for rehire status

**Education Verification**
- Degree and certification confirmation
- Institution accreditation verification
- GPA verification (if required)
- Professional license validation

### Enhanced Background Checks
**Criminal History**
- County, state, and federal records
- Sex offender registry check
- International criminal records (if applicable)
- Ongoing monitoring for sensitive positions

**Financial Background**
- Credit history review (for financial roles)
- Bankruptcy and lien searches
- Asset verification (for executive positions)
- Conflict of interest screening

### Reference Checks
**Professional References**
- Minimum three professional contacts
- Former supervisors and colleagues
- Client or customer references (if applicable)
- Industry peer recommendations

**Reference Interview Topics**
- Work performance and quality
- Reliability and attendance
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Areas for improvement
- Rehire recommendation

## Offer and Negotiation

### Offer Components
**Compensation Package**
- Base salary within approved range
- Variable compensation (if applicable)
- Equity or stock options
- Sign-on bonus (for exceptional candidates)

**Benefits Package**
- Health, dental, and vision insurance
- Retirement plan participation
- Paid time off allocation
- Professional development budget
- Flexible work arrangements

### Negotiation Guidelines
**Approved Negotiation Range**
- Salary: Up to 10% above midpoint
- Start date: Within 4 weeks
- Additional PTO: Up to 5 extra days
- Professional development: Up to $2,000 annually

**Non-Negotiable Items**
- Core benefits structure
- Company policies and procedures
- Performance review process
- Confidentiality and non-compete agreements

## Compliance and Legal Requirements

### Documentation Requirements
**Retention Periods**
- Applications and resumes: 1 year
- Interview notes and evaluations: 2 years
- Background check results: 7 years
- Offer letters and contracts: Permanent

**I-9 Compliance**
- Form completion within 3 days
- Document verification and copying
- E-Verify submission (if required)
- Regular audit and maintenance

### Reporting and Analytics

- Source effectiveness measurement

**Compliance Monitoring**
- EEO-1 report preparation
- OFCCP compliance (if applicable)
- State and local reporting requirements
- Internal audit and review processes
