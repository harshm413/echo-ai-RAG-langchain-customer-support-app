# Workplace Harassment Prevention Policy

## Policy Statement

Our company is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of harassment and are dedicated to creating an inclusive, respectful environment for all employees.

## Scope and Application

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and temporary workers
- All company premises and work-related locations
- Company-sponsored events and activities
- Business travel and off-site meetings
- Digital communications and social media interactions
- Remote work environments and virtual meetings

## Definitions

### Harassment
Unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics that:
- Creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment
- Interferes with an individual's work performance
- Affects employment decisions or opportunities
- Is severe or pervasive enough to alter working conditions

### Protected Characteristics
- Race, color, ethnicity, national origin
- Religion, creed, or spiritual beliefs
- Sex, gender, gender identity, gender expression
- Sexual orientation
- Age (40 and older)
- Disability or medical condition
- Pregnancy, childbirth, or related conditions
- Veteran or military status
- Genetic information
- Any other legally protected status

### Sexual Harassment
Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or other verbal, physical, or visual conduct of a sexual nature when:
- Submission is made a condition of employment
- Submission or rejection affects employment decisions
- Conduct creates an intimidating or hostile work environment

## Types of Prohibited Conduct

### Verbal Harassment
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or epithets
- Derogatory comments or name-calling
- Threats or intimidation
- Unwelcome sexual comments or propositions
- Inappropriate questions about personal life

### Physical Harassment
- Unwanted touching, hugging, or kissing
- Blocking movement or cornering
- Physical assault or threats of violence
- Inappropriate gestures or invasion of personal space
- Destruction of personal property

### Visual Harassment
- Displaying offensive images, cartoons, or materials
- Inappropriate emails, texts, or social media posts
- Lewd gestures or facial expressions
- Staring or ogling
- Graffiti or written materials

### Digital Harassment
- Cyberbullying or online intimidation
- Inappropriate social media interactions
- Unwanted digital communications
- Sharing private information without consent
- Creating fake profiles or impersonation

## Reporting Procedures

### Multiple Reporting Options
Employees can report harassment through any of the following channels:
- Direct supervisor or manager
- Human Resources department
- Anonymous hotline: 1-800-REPORT-IT
- Online reporting portal
- Any member of senior leadership
- Third-party ombudsman service

### Reporting Information
When reporting harassment, please provide:
- Names of individuals involved
- Dates, times, and locations of incidents
- Description of what occurred
- Names of witnesses (if any)
- Any supporting documentation
- Previous attempts to address the issue

### Anonymous Reporting
- Anonymous reports are accepted and investigated
- Follow-up may be limited without contact information
- Encourage providing contact details for thorough investigation
- Protection measures available for anonymous reporters

## Investigation Process

### Immediate Response
**Within 24 Hours**
- Acknowledge receipt of complaint
- Assess need for interim protective measures
- Assign qualified investigator
- Notify appropriate stakeholders
- Document initial response actions

**Interim Measures**
- Temporary separation of parties
- Schedule or location adjustments
- Modified reporting relationships
- Paid administrative leave (if necessary)
- Counseling and support resources

### Investigation Procedures
**Investigator Qualifications**
- Trained in harassment investigation techniques
- Impartial and free from conflicts of interest
- Appropriate level of authority
- Understanding of legal requirements

**Investigation Steps**
1. Interview complainant in detail
2. Interview alleged harasser
3. Interview witnesses and relevant parties
4. Review relevant documents and evidence
5. Analyze findings and determine credibility
6. Prepare comprehensive investigation report

### Timeline and Communication
- Investigation completed within 30 business days
- Regular updates provided to complainant
- Extensions communicated if additional time needed
- Final outcome notification to all parties
- Documentation maintained confidentially

## Resolution and Corrective Action

### Substantiated Findings
When harassment is confirmed, corrective action may include:

**Progressive Discipline**
- Verbal warning and counseling
- Written warning and training
- Suspension without pay
- Demotion or transfer
- Termination of employment

**Remedial Measures**
- Mandatory harassment training
- Coaching and mentoring
- Performance monitoring
- Behavioral agreements
- Restoration of lost opportunities

### Unsubstantiated Findings
- No disciplinary action against accused
- Continued monitoring of workplace dynamics
- Additional training or education (if appropriate)
- Support resources for all parties
- Documentation of investigation process

### False Accusations
- Distinction between unsubstantiated and false claims
- Disciplinary action for knowingly false reports
- Protection for good faith reporting
- Consideration of impact on accused individual

## Retaliation Prevention

### Prohibited Retaliation
Retaliation is strictly prohibited against individuals who:
- Report harassment in good faith
- Participate in harassment investigations
- Oppose discriminatory practices
- Exercise rights under this policy

### Forms of Retaliation
- Adverse employment actions
- Exclusion from meetings or opportunities
- Increased scrutiny or criticism
- Social isolation or ostracism
- Threats or intimidation

### Reporting Retaliation
- Same reporting channels as harassment
- Immediate investigation of retaliation claims
- Swift corrective action if retaliation confirmed
- Protection measures for affected individuals

## Manager and Supervisor Responsibilities

### Prevention Duties
- Model appropriate behavior and communication
- Create inclusive and respectful team environment
- Address inappropriate conduct immediately
- Provide regular team training and education
- Monitor workplace dynamics and relationships

### Response Obligations
- Take all complaints seriously and act promptly
- Report harassment allegations to HR immediately
- Cooperate fully with investigation processes
- Implement interim protective measures
- Follow up on resolution effectiveness

### Documentation Requirements
- Maintain detailed records of incidents and responses
- Document training completion and acknowledgments
- Record performance and behavioral issues
- Preserve evidence and witness statements
- Ensure confidential handling of sensitive information

## Confidentiality and Privacy

### Information Protection
- Limit disclosure to those with need to know
- Maintain secure storage of investigation files
- Protect identity of reporters and witnesses
- Balance confidentiality with investigation needs
- Comply with legal disclosure requirements

### Privacy Considerations
- Respect dignity of all parties involved
- Minimize disruption to work environment
- Protect against gossip and speculation
- Maintain professional boundaries
- Consider impact on reputation and relationships
