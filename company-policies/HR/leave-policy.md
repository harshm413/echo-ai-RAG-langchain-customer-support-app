# Leave Policy

## Overview

This policy outlines the various types of leave available to employees and the procedures for requesting and managing leave time.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Accrual Rates
Leave entitlement is based on years of service:

**Tier 1 (0-3 Years of Service)**
- 15 days per annum
- Accrual rate: 1.25 days per month
- Maximum carryover: 5 days

**Tier 2 (3-5 Years of Service)**
- 20 days per annum
- Accrual rate: 1.67 days per month
- Maximum carryover: 7 days

**Tier 3 (5+ Years of Service)**
- 25 days per annum
- Accrual rate: 2.08 days per month
- Maximum carryover: 10 days

### PTO Usage Guidelines
- Minimum increment: Half-day (4 hours)
- Advance notice required: 2 weeks for planned leave
- Emergency leave: Notify supervisor within 24 hours
- Blackout periods apply during critical business periods

## Sick Leave

### Entitlement
- 10 days per year for all employees
- Accrues at 0.83 days per month
- No carryover to following year
- Can be used for personal illness or family care

### Usage Requirements
- Medical certification required for absences over 3 consecutive days
- Return-to-work clearance may be required
- Intermittent use allowed for chronic conditions

## Family and Medical Leave

### Eligibility
- 12 months of employment
- 1,250 hours worked in previous 12 months
- Up to 12 weeks unpaid leave per year

### Qualifying Events
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Serious health condition of employee or family member
- Military family leave
- Qualifying exigency leave

### Application Process
- 30 days advance notice when foreseeable
- Medical certification required
- Periodic recertification may be requested
- Job restoration guaranteed upon return

## Bereavement Leave

### Immediate Family
- Up to 5 days paid leave
- Includes spouse, children, parents, siblings
- Additional unpaid leave may be granted

### Extended Family
- Up to 3 days paid leave
- Includes grandparents, in-laws, aunts, uncles
- Manager approval required

## Jury Duty and Court Appearances

### Jury Duty
- Paid leave for jury service
- Must provide court summons
- Return to work if dismissed early

### Court Appearances
- Unpaid leave for personal legal matters
- Paid leave if subpoenaed as witness
- Advance notice required

## Military Leave

### Reserve Duty
- Unpaid leave for training and service
- Job protection guaranteed
- Benefits continuation available

### Deployment
- Extended unpaid leave
- Reemployment rights protected
- Health insurance continuation

## Leave Request Process

### Advance Planning
1. Submit request through HR system
2. Manager review and approval
3. HR verification of available balance
4. Confirmation sent to employee

### Emergency Leave
1. Notify supervisor immediately
2. Submit formal request within 48 hours
3. Provide documentation if required
4. Follow up with HR department

## Leave Without Pay

### Eligibility
- Exhausted all paid leave options
- Manager and HR approval required
- Maximum 30 days per year

### Conditions
- Benefits continuation at employee expense
- No accrual of benefits during unpaid leave
- Return date must be confirmed

## Sabbatical Leave

### Eligibility
- 7+ years of continuous service
- Exceptional performance record
- Business needs permit absence

### Terms
- Up to 6 months unpaid leave
- Partial benefits continuation
- Development plan required
- Return commitment agreement

## Holiday Schedule

### Observed Holidays
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve

### Floating Holidays
- 2 additional days per year
- Must be used in calendar year
- Manager approval required

## Return from Leave

### Requirements
- Medical clearance if applicable
- Confirmation of return date
- Update emergency contacts
- Review any policy changes

### Reintegration
- Gradual return schedule if needed
- Training on new procedures
- Performance goal adjustment
- Support resources available

## Policy Violations

### Consequences
- Unauthorized absence: Disciplinary action
- Fraudulent leave use: Termination
- Failure to return: Job abandonment
- Documentation falsification: Immediate termination
