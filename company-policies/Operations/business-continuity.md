# Business Continuity Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes our organization's commitment to maintaining critical business operations during and after disruptive events, ensuring the safety of personnel, protection of assets, and continuation of essential services to customers and stakeholders.

## Business Continuity Framework

### Business Continuity Objectives
- **Life Safety**: Protect employees, customers, and visitors from harm
- **Asset Protection**: Safeguard physical and information assets
- **Service Continuity**: Maintain critical business functions and services
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Provide timely and accurate information
- **Recovery Management**: Restore normal operations efficiently
- **Reputation Protection**: Maintain organizational credibility and trust

### Business Continuity Governance
**Business Continuity Committee**
- Executive sponsor and leadership team
- Business continuity coordinator
- Department representatives
- IT and facilities management
- Communications and legal representatives

**Roles and Responsibilities**
- Executive leadership: Strategic direction and resource allocation
- Business continuity coordinator: Program management and coordination
- Department managers: Departmental planning and execution
- Employees: Plan awareness and compliance
- External partners: Support and coordination

## Business Impact Analysis

### Critical Business Functions
**Tier 1 - Critical (RTO: 0-4 hours)**
- Customer service and support operations
- Core production and service delivery
- Financial transactions and payments
- IT infrastructure and data systems
- Safety and security operations

**Tier 2 - Important (RTO: 4-24 hours)**
- Sales and marketing activities
- Human resources operations
- Procurement and supply chain
- Quality assurance and compliance
- Administrative and support functions

**Tier 3 - Necessary (RTO: 24-72 hours)**
- Training and development programs
- Strategic planning and analysis
- Non-critical reporting and analytics
- Facilities maintenance and operations
- Archive and records management

### Impact Assessment Criteria
**Financial Impact**
- Revenue loss and opportunity cost
- Additional expense and recovery costs
- Contractual penalties and fines
- Insurance deductibles and claims
- Market share and competitive position

**Operational Impact**
- Service disruption and delays
- Customer dissatisfaction and complaints
- Regulatory compliance violations
- Supply chain disruptions
- Employee productivity loss

**Reputational Impact**
- Brand damage and negative publicity
- Customer confidence and trust
- Stakeholder relationships
- Market perception and credibility
- Long-term business relationships
