# Workplace Safety Policy

## Policy Statement and Commitment

Our company is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for all employees, contractors, visitors, and stakeholders. We believe that all workplace injuries and illnesses are preventable and that safety is everyone's responsibility.

## Safety Management System

### Safety Leadership
**Management Commitment**
- Executive leadership actively promotes safety culture
- Adequate resources allocated for safety programs
- Regular safety performance review and improvement
- Integration of safety into business planning
- Visible leadership participation in safety activities

**Safety Governance Structure**
- Safety Committee with cross-functional representation
- Designated Safety Officers for each department
- Regular safety meetings and communications
- Safety performance metrics and reporting
- Continuous improvement and corrective action programs

### Employee Responsibilities
**All Employees Must**
- Follow all safety policies and procedures
- Report unsafe conditions and near-miss incidents
- Use required personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Participate in safety training and drills
- Take reasonable care for their own safety and others

**Supervisors and Managers Must**
- Ensure safe work practices in their areas
- Provide safety training and resources
- Investigate incidents and implement corrective actions
- Conduct regular safety inspections
- Model safe behavior and enforce safety rules

## Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment

### Workplace Hazard Categories
**Physical Hazards**
- Slips, trips, and falls
- Electrical hazards and equipment
- Fire and explosion risks
- Noise and vibration exposure
- Temperature extremes and weather

**Chemical Hazards**
- Cleaning chemicals and solvents
- Office supplies and materials
- Air quality and ventilation
- Hazardous waste and disposal
- Chemical storage and handling

**Biological Hazards**
- Infectious diseases and pathogens
- Indoor air quality issues
- Mold and allergen exposure
- Food safety and hygiene
- Pest control and management

**Ergonomic Hazards**
- Repetitive motion injuries
- Poor workstation design
- Heavy lifting and manual handling
- Prolonged sitting or standing
- Eye strain and visual fatigue

**Psychosocial Hazards**
- Workplace stress and pressure
- Harassment and violence
- Work-life balance issues
- Job insecurity and change
- Interpersonal conflicts

### Risk Assessment Process
**Hazard Identification**
- Regular workplace inspections
- Employee hazard reporting
- Incident and near-miss analysis
- Job safety analysis (JSA)
- Safety audit findings

**Risk Evaluation**
- Likelihood and severity assessment
- Risk matrix and scoring
- Exposure frequency and duration
- Vulnerable population identification
- Regulatory compliance requirements

**Control Measures**
- Elimination of hazards when possible
- Substitution with safer alternatives
- Engineering controls and safeguards
- Administrative controls and procedures
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Incident Management

### Incident Reporting
**Reportable Incidents**
- All injuries requiring medical treatment
- Near-miss events and close calls
- Property damage and equipment failures
- Environmental releases or spills
- Security breaches and threats

**Reporting Procedures**
- Immediate supervisor notification
- Safety department reporting within 24 hours
- Incident report form completion
- Witness statements and evidence collection
- Regulatory reporting when required

### Incident Investigation
**Investigation Process**
- Immediate scene securing and preservation
- Investigation team assignment
- Root cause analysis methodology
- Contributing factor identification
- Corrective action development

**Investigation Documentation**
- Detailed incident report preparation
- Photographic evidence and documentation
- Interview records and statements
- Timeline and sequence of events
- Lessons learned and recommendations

### Corrective Actions
**Immediate Actions**
- Medical treatment and care
- Hazard elimination or control
- Equipment isolation or repair
- Temporary safety measures
- Employee notification and communication

**Long-term Improvements**
- Policy and procedure updates
- Training program enhancements
- Engineering controls implementation
- Management system improvements
- Performance monitoring and measurement

**Work Practice Improvements**
- Regular breaks and stretching
- Job rotation and task variation
- Proper lifting techniques
- Workload management and pacing
- Employee training and awareness
