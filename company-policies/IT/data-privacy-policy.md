# Data Privacy Policy

## Purpose and Commitment

This policy demonstrates our commitment to protecting the privacy and personal data of our employees, customers, partners, and stakeholders. We are dedicated to transparent, lawful, and ethical data processing practices that respect individual privacy rights.

## Scope and Application

### Covered Data Types
**Personal Data**
- Names, addresses, and contact information
- Identification numbers and documents
- Financial and payment information
- Employment and professional data
- Health and medical information
- Biometric and genetic data
- Online identifiers and digital footprints

**Special Categories of Data**
- Racial or ethnic origin
- Political opinions and affiliations
- Religious or philosophical beliefs
- Trade union membership
- Health and medical data
- Sexual orientation and preferences
- Criminal convictions and offenses

### Covered Individuals
- Current and former employees
- Job applicants and candidates
- Customers and clients
- Vendors and business partners
- Website visitors and users
- Emergency contacts and beneficiaries

## Legal Framework and Compliance

### Applicable Regulations
**International Standards**
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
- Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD)
- Other applicable local data protection laws

**Industry Standards**
- ISO 27001 Information Security Management
- ISO 27701 Privacy Information Management
- NIST Privacy Framework
- SOC 2 Type II compliance requirements

### Data Protection Principles
**Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**
- Process data lawfully and fairly
- Provide clear information about data processing
- Ensure transparency in all data handling activities
- Obtain appropriate consent when required

**Purpose Limitation**
- Collect data for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
- Do not process data for incompatible purposes
- Clearly communicate processing purposes to individuals
- Regularly review and validate processing purposes

**Data Minimization**
- Collect only data that is necessary and relevant
- Limit data collection to what is required for the purpose
- Regularly review data holdings and delete unnecessary data
- Implement privacy by design principles

**Accuracy**
- Ensure personal data is accurate and up-to-date
- Implement processes to correct inaccurate data
- Regularly review and update data records
- Provide mechanisms for individuals to update their data

**Storage Limitation**
- Retain data only as long as necessary
- Implement clear data retention schedules
- Securely delete data when no longer needed
- Regular review of data retention practices

**Integrity and Confidentiality**
- Implement appropriate security measures
- Protect data against unauthorized access and processing
- Ensure data confidentiality and integrity
- Regular security assessments and improvements

**Accountability**
- Demonstrate compliance with data protection principles
- Maintain comprehensive records of processing activities
- Implement privacy governance and oversight
- Regular training and awareness programs

## Data Collection and Processing

### Lawful Bases for Processing
**Consent**
- Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous
- Clear affirmative action required
- Right to withdraw consent at any time
- Separate consent for different processing purposes

**Contract Performance**
- Processing necessary for contract performance
- Pre-contractual measures at individual's request
- Employment contract obligations
- Service delivery requirements

**Legal Obligation**
- Compliance with legal or regulatory requirements
- Court orders and legal proceedings
- Tax and financial reporting obligations
- Health and safety requirements

**Vital Interests**
- Protection of life or physical safety
- Emergency situations and medical care
- Public health and safety concerns
- Critical infrastructure protection

**Public Task**
- Performance of public interest tasks
- Exercise of official authority
- Regulatory compliance and reporting
- Public safety and security

**Legitimate Interests**
- Balancing test conducted and documented
- Individual rights and freedoms considered
- Transparent communication about processing
- Regular review of legitimate interest assessments

## Individual Rights and Requests

### Right to Information
**Transparency Requirements**
- Clear and plain language privacy notices
- Information about processing purposes and legal bases
- Data retention periods and deletion practices
- Individual rights and how to exercise them
- Contact information for privacy inquiries

### Right of Access
**Subject Access Requests**
- Confirmation of data processing
- Copy of personal data being processed
- Information about processing purposes and recipients
- Data retention periods and sources
- Response within one month of request

### Right to Rectification
**Data Correction**
- Correction of inaccurate personal data
- Completion of incomplete data
- Notification to third parties when appropriate
- Prompt response to correction requests
- Documentation of corrections made

### Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)
**Deletion Circumstances**
- Data no longer necessary for original purpose
- Consent withdrawn and no other lawful basis
- Data processed unlawfully
- Legal obligation to delete data
- Objection to processing with no overriding interests

### Right to Restrict Processing
**Processing Restrictions**
- Accuracy of data is contested
- Processing is unlawful but deletion not wanted
- Data no longer needed but required for legal claims
- Objection to processing pending verification
- Limited processing activities during restriction

### Right to Data Portability
**Data Transfer**
- Structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
- Direct transmission to another controller when possible
- Applies to automated processing based on consent or contract
- Does not adversely affect others' rights and freedoms

### Right to Object
**Objection Grounds**
- Processing based on legitimate interests or public task
- Direct marketing activities
- Scientific, historical, or statistical research
- Automated decision-making and profiling
- Compelling legitimate grounds required to continue processing

### Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making
**Automated Processing Protections**
- Right not to be subject to automated decision-making
- Human intervention in decision-making processes
- Right to contest automated decisions
- Explanation of decision-making logic and consequences

## Data Retention and Disposal

### Retention Schedules
**Employee Data**
- Active employment: Duration of employment plus 7 years
- Recruitment data: 2 years from application date
- Performance records: 7 years after employment ends
- Payroll records: 7 years for tax compliance
- Training records: Duration of employment plus 3 years

**Customer Data**
- Active customers: Duration of relationship plus 7 years
- Inactive customers: 3 years from last interaction
- Marketing data: Until consent withdrawn or 3 years
- Transaction records: 7 years for financial compliance
- Support records: 3 years from case closure

**Business Records**
- Financial records: 7 years for regulatory compliance
- Contract records: Duration of contract plus 7 years
- Legal records: As required by legal obligations
- Audit records: 7 years from audit completion
- Security logs: 2 years for incident investigation

### Secure Disposal
**Data Destruction Methods**
- Secure deletion of electronic data
- Physical destruction of storage media
- Certificate of destruction for sensitive data
- Verification of complete data removal
- Documentation of disposal activities
