# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable use of company information technology resources, including computers, networks, software, and data. All employees, contractors, and authorized users must comply with this policy.

## Covered Resources

### Technology Assets
- Desktop and laptop computers
- Mobile devices (smartphones, tablets)
- Servers and network infrastructure
- Software applications and licenses
- Email and communication systems
- Internet and network access
- Cloud services and storage
- Printers and peripheral devices

### Information Assets
- Company data and databases
- Customer information and records
- Intellectual property and trade secrets
- Financial and business information
- Employee personal information
- System logs and security data

## Acceptable Use Guidelines

### General Principles
**Professional Use**
- Primary use for business purposes
- Support of company objectives and operations
- Compliance with all applicable laws and regulations
- Respect for intellectual property rights
- Protection of confidential information

**Personal Use**
- Limited personal use permitted during breaks and lunch
- No interference with work responsibilities
- No excessive bandwidth consumption
- No storage of large personal files
- Compliance with all security policies

### Email and Communication
**Appropriate Email Use**
- Professional tone and language
- Clear and concise communication
- Appropriate use of company signature
- Timely response to business communications
- Proper handling of confidential information

**Email Retention**
- Comply with company retention policies
- Delete unnecessary emails regularly
- Archive important business communications
- Use appropriate folder organization
- Backup critical email data

### Internet and Web Browsing
**Acceptable Internet Use**
- Business research and information gathering
- Professional development and training
- Industry news and market intelligence
- Vendor and supplier communications
- Limited personal use during breaks

**Web Browsing Guidelines**
- Avoid sites with inappropriate content
- No downloading of unauthorized software
- Be cautious of phishing and malicious sites
- Report suspicious websites to IT security
- Use company-approved browsers and settings

## Prohibited Activities

### Security Violations
**Unauthorized Access**
- Attempting to access restricted systems or data
- Sharing login credentials with others
- Using another person's account or password
- Bypassing security controls or authentication
- Accessing systems beyond authorized scope

**Malicious Activities**
- Installing malware or viruses
- Hacking or attempting to compromise systems
- Creating or distributing harmful code
- Participating in denial-of-service attacks
- Unauthorized network scanning or probing

### Inappropriate Content
**Prohibited Content Types**
- Pornographic or sexually explicit material
- Violent or disturbing content
- Hate speech or discriminatory material
- Illegal or criminal content
- Gambling or gaming sites (during work hours)

**Content Distribution**
- Forwarding chain letters or spam
- Distributing copyrighted material without permission
- Sharing inappropriate jokes or images
- Posting confidential company information
- Creating or sharing defamatory content

### Misuse of Resources
**Resource Abuse**
- Excessive personal use of company resources
- Using systems for personal business ventures
- Cryptocurrency mining or trading
- Streaming media during work hours
- Storing large personal files on company systems

**Bandwidth Abuse**
- Downloading large personal files
- Streaming video or audio for entertainment
- File sharing of copyrighted material
- Running peer-to-peer applications
- Excessive social media use during work hours

## Software and Licensing

### Software Installation
**Approved Software**
- Only IT-approved software may be installed
- Request approval through IT service desk
- Provide business justification for software needs
- Comply with licensing terms and conditions
- Regular software inventory and compliance checks

**Prohibited Software**
- Unlicensed or pirated software
- Personal software on company devices
- Peer-to-peer file sharing applications
- Unauthorized remote access tools
- Software with known security vulnerabilities

### License Compliance
**Software Licensing**
- Use software only within license terms
- Do not exceed licensed user counts
- Maintain accurate software inventory
- Report license violations immediately
- Participate in software audit processes

## Data Protection and Privacy

### Data Handling
**Confidential Information**
- Access only data necessary for job functions
- Protect confidential information from unauthorized disclosure
- Use encryption for sensitive data transmission
- Store confidential data in approved locations only
- Report data breaches immediately

**Personal Information**
- Respect privacy of employee and customer data
- Comply with data protection regulations
- Obtain consent before collecting personal information
- Use personal data only for authorized purposes
- Implement appropriate data retention practices

### Data Storage and Backup
**Approved Storage Locations**
- Use company-approved cloud storage services
- Store business data on company servers
- Avoid storing sensitive data on local devices
- Regular backup of critical business data
- Secure disposal of data when no longer needed

## Mobile Device Usage

### Company-Owned Devices
**Device Management**
- Install required security software and updates
- Use strong passwords or biometric authentication
- Enable automatic screen locks
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately
- Allow remote wipe capabilities

**Usage Guidelines**
- Primary use for business purposes
- Limited personal use permitted
- No jailbreaking or rooting devices
- Install apps only from approved sources
- Comply with mobile device management policies

### Personal Devices (BYOD)
**Security Requirements**
- Enroll in mobile device management system
- Install required security applications
- Maintain current operating system and security patches
- Use separate containers for business and personal data
- Allow company data to be remotely wiped

## Social Media and External Communications

### Social Media Guidelines
**Personal Social Media**
- Do not represent yourself as speaking for the company
- Avoid posting confidential company information
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions
- Disclose company affiliation when discussing industry topics
- Report any negative comments about the company

**Company Social Media**
- Only authorized personnel may post on company accounts
- Follow company branding and messaging guidelines
- Obtain approval for significant announcements
- Monitor and respond to comments appropriately
- Maintain professional tone and image

### External Communications
**Public Speaking and Writing**
- Obtain approval before speaking at industry events
- Disclose company affiliation in professional writings
- Avoid sharing confidential or proprietary information
- Ensure consistency with company messaging
- Report media inquiries to communications team

## Remote Work and Telecommuting

### Home Office Security
**Network Security**
- Use secure, password-protected Wi-Fi networks
- Avoid public Wi-Fi for business communications
- Use VPN for all remote access to company systems
- Keep home networks updated with security patches
- Separate business and personal network traffic

**Physical Security**
- Secure company equipment when not in use
- Prevent unauthorized access to company devices
- Use privacy screens in public locations
- Lock devices when stepping away
- Store equipment securely overnight

### Remote Access
**VPN Usage**
- Connect to VPN before accessing company resources
- Use only company-approved VPN clients
- Maintain VPN connection throughout work session
- Report VPN connectivity issues to IT support
- Do not share VPN credentials with others

## Monitoring and Privacy

### System Monitoring
**Monitoring Scope**
- All company IT resources are subject to monitoring
- Email, internet, and application usage may be logged
- Security monitoring for threat detection
- Performance monitoring for system optimization
- Compliance monitoring for policy adherence

**Privacy Expectations**
- Limited privacy expectation on company systems
- Personal communications may be monitored
- Monitoring conducted in accordance with applicable laws
- Employee notification of monitoring activities
- Data retention according to company policies

### Audit and Investigation
**Audit Rights**
- Company reserves right to audit all IT resources
- Investigations may be conducted for policy violations
- Cooperation required during audit processes
- Evidence preservation for legal proceedings
- External audit support when necessary

## Incident Reporting

### Reporting Requirements
**Security Incidents**
- Report suspected security breaches immediately
- Document all relevant details and circumstances
- Preserve evidence and avoid system changes
- Cooperate with investigation processes
- Follow incident response procedures

**Policy Violations**
- Report observed policy violations
- Use appropriate reporting channels
- Maintain confidentiality during investigations
- Provide truthful and complete information
- Avoid retaliation against reporters

## Policy Violations and Consequences

### Violation Categories
**Minor Violations**
- Occasional personal use beyond guidelines
- Failure to install required updates
- Minor policy oversights
- First-time violations with no harm

**Major Violations**
- Intentional security policy violations
- Unauthorized access to confidential data
- Installation of prohibited software
- Repeated policy violations

**Severe Violations**
- Malicious activities or sabotage
- Data theft or unauthorized disclosure
- Harassment or illegal activities
- Violations causing significant harm

### Disciplinary Actions
**Progressive Discipline**
- Verbal warning and counseling
- Written warning and additional training
- Suspension and performance improvement
- Termination of employment
- Legal action (if applicable)

### Appeal Process
**Violation Appeals**
- Right to appeal disciplinary actions
- Formal appeal process through HR
- Independent review of evidence
- Fair and impartial hearing
- Final decision documentation
