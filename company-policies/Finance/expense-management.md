# Expense Management Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes guidelines for business expense reporting, reimbursement procedures, and corporate credit card usage to ensure appropriate use of company funds while supporting legitimate business activities.

## Policy Overview

### Guiding Principles
- All business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business
- Expenses must be properly documented with original receipts and business justification
- Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and seek cost-effective solutions
- Personal expenses are not reimbursable under any circumstances
- All expense reports must be submitted timely and accurately

### Covered Expenses
**Reimbursable Business Expenses**
- Travel and transportation costs
- Meals and entertainment for business purposes
- Lodging and accommodation expenses
- Conference and training fees
- Office supplies and equipment
- Client gifts and promotional items
- Professional development and education
- Business communication expenses

## Travel and Transportation

### Air Travel
**Booking Requirements**
- Use company-preferred travel agency or booking platform
- Book flights at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Choose economy class for domestic flights under 4 hours
- Business class permitted for international flights over 6 hours
- First class requires executive approval

**Airline Policies**
- Use company-preferred airlines when available
- Frequent flyer miles earned on business travel belong to employee
- Seat upgrades at personal expense unless medically necessary
- Travel insurance recommended for international trips
- Cancel or change flights promptly to avoid penalties

### Ground Transportation
**Rental Cars**
- Mid-size or smaller vehicles unless business need requires larger
- Use company-preferred rental agencies for corporate rates
- Decline rental car insurance if covered by company policy
- Fuel receipts required for reimbursement
- Personal use of rental cars not permitted

**Other Transportation**
- Taxi, rideshare, and public transportation reimbursable
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use at IRS rates
- Parking fees and tolls reimbursable with receipts
- Airport shuttle and transportation services covered
- Excessive transportation costs require justification

### Lodging and Accommodation
**Hotel Selection**
- Use company-preferred hotels when available
- Choose reasonably priced accommodations appropriate for location
- Single occupancy rooms standard for business travel
- Extended stay discounts should be utilized for long trips
- Personal guests or room upgrades at employee expense

**Accommodation Guidelines**
- Book accommodations close to business location when possible
- Consider total cost including transportation to/from hotel
- Incidental charges (movies, minibar, spa) not reimbursable
- Internet and business center charges reimbursable
- Early departure fees avoided when possible

## Meals and Entertainment

### Business Meals
**Meal Allowances**
- Breakfast: $25 per day maximum
- Lunch: $35 per day maximum  
- Dinner: $75 per day maximum
- Receipts required for all meals over $25
- Alcohol limited to reasonable amounts with business meals

**Business Entertainment**
**Client Entertainment**
- Pre-approval required for entertainment over $200 per person
- Must include legitimate business discussion
- Attendees and business purpose must be documented
- Alcohol permitted in moderation
- Excessive or lavish entertainment not permitted

**Employee Entertainment**
- Team building and recognition events approved
- Holiday parties and company celebrations covered
- Spouse/partner inclusion requires approval
- Reasonable limits based on number of attendees
- Documentation of business purpose required

### Meal Guidelines
**Travel Meals**
- Meals included in conference or event registration not reimbursable separately
- Room service permitted when business schedule requires
- Meals during local travel (under 50 miles) generally not reimbursable
- Group meals require itemized receipts and attendee list
- Tips limited to 20% of meal cost

## Conference and Training

### Professional Development
**Approved Training**
- Job-related training and certification programs
- Industry conferences and professional meetings
- Skills development and continuing education
- Leadership and management training
- Technical and software training

**Approval Process**
- Manager approval required for all training expenses
- Budget approval for expenses over $2,500
- Training plan and objectives documented
- Post-training report or knowledge sharing required
- Cancellation policies reviewed before registration

### Conference Expenses
**Registration and Fees**
- Early bird registration rates preferred
- Group discounts utilized when available
- Membership discounts applied when applicable
- Cancellation insurance considered for expensive events
- Substitute attendees permitted with advance notice

## Office Supplies and Equipment

### Allowable Purchases
**Office Supplies**
- Standard office supplies and materials
- Computer accessories and peripherals
- Reference materials and publications
- Software licenses and subscriptions
- Communication and presentation equipment

**Equipment Guidelines**
- IT approval required for technology purchases over $500
- Ergonomic equipment for health and safety needs
- Furniture and office setup items
- Safety equipment and supplies
- Tools and equipment specific to job function

### Purchase Procedures
**Small Purchases (Under $100)**
- Direct purchase with personal funds and reimbursement
- Receipt and business justification required
- Manager approval through expense report
- Petty cash available for immediate needs

**Larger Purchases ($100-$1,000)**
- Purchase order or company credit card preferred
- Multiple quotes for purchases over $500
- Manager approval before purchase
- Asset tagging and inventory requirements
- Warranty and support documentation retained

## Corporate Credit Card Usage

### Card Issuance
**Eligibility Requirements**
- Regular business travel or purchasing responsibilities
- Manager recommendation and approval
- Completion of credit card training
- Signed cardholder agreement
- Good credit history and financial standing

**Card Types**
- Individual liability cards for most employees
- Corporate liability cards for senior executives
- Purchasing cards for procurement activities
- Travel cards for frequent travelers
- Temporary cards for specific projects or events

### Usage Guidelines
**Appropriate Uses**
- Business travel and entertainment expenses
- Office supplies and equipment purchases
- Conference registration and training fees
- Client gifts and promotional materials
- Emergency business expenses

**Prohibited Uses**
- Personal purchases of any kind
- Cash advances except in emergencies
- Alcohol purchases outside of business meals
- Gift cards or cash equivalents
- Personal vehicle fuel (unless rental car)
- Family or personal travel expenses

### Card Management
**Monthly Reconciliation**
- Review and approve all charges monthly
- Submit receipts and documentation promptly
- Dispute unauthorized or incorrect charges
- Report lost or stolen cards immediately
- Maintain card security and confidentiality

**Compliance Requirements**
- Expense reports submitted within 30 days
- Original receipts for all charges over $25
- Business purpose documented for all expenses
- Manager approval for expense reports
- Corporate card statements reconciled monthly

## Expense Reporting Procedures

### Submission Requirements
**Timing**
- Expense reports submitted within 30 days of expense
- Monthly submission required for corporate card holders
- Final expense reports due within 60 days of trip completion
- Late submissions may result in delayed reimbursement
- Outstanding reports prevent new travel approvals

**Documentation**
- Original receipts for all expenses over $25
- Credit card receipts and statements
- Detailed business purpose and attendees
- Mileage logs for vehicle reimbursement
- Foreign currency conversion documentation

### Expense Report Content
**Required Information**
- Employee name and department
- Expense dates and locations
- Vendor names and addresses
- Business purpose and attendees
- Project or client codes (if applicable)
- Manager approval and authorization

**Supporting Documentation**
- Itemized receipts (not just credit card slips)
- Hotel folios showing zero balance
- Airline tickets or boarding passes
- Conference agendas and registration confirmations
- Mileage calculation worksheets

### Approval Process
**Manager Review**
- Verify business purpose and necessity
- Confirm policy compliance
- Review supporting documentation
- Approve or reject with explanation
- Forward to accounting for processing

**Accounting Review**
- Verify mathematical accuracy
- Confirm receipt requirements met
- Check policy compliance
- Process approved reimbursements
- Return incomplete reports for correction

## Reimbursement Procedures

### Processing Timeline
**Standard Reimbursement**
- Approved expense reports processed within 5 business days
- Direct deposit to employee bank account
- Reimbursement included in next payroll cycle
- Email notification when payment processed
- Detailed payment stub provided

**Expedited Processing**
- Available for hardship situations
- Manager and HR approval required
- Processing within 2 business days
- Additional documentation may be required
- Limited to essential business expenses

### Payment Methods
**Direct Deposit**
- Primary reimbursement method
- Bank account information on file required
- Secure electronic transfer
- Detailed payment notification
- No processing fees or delays

**Check Payment**
- Available when direct deposit not possible
- Mailed to employee home address
- Additional processing time required
- Stop payment fees if check lost
- Reissue process for lost checks

## International Travel and Expenses

### Currency and Exchange Rates
**Foreign Currency**
- USD conversion required for all expenses
- Exchange rates from date of transaction
- Credit card conversion rates acceptable
- Currency conversion receipts retained
- Large cash exchanges documented

**International Considerations**
- Visa and passport fees reimbursable
- International phone and data charges covered
- Cultural gift-giving customs respected
- Local tax and service charges included
- Travel health insurance recommended

### Tax Implications
**VAT and Tax Recovery**
- Business VAT recovery when possible
- Original tax invoices required
- Local tax advisor consultation available
- Documentation for tax authorities
- Compliance with local tax laws

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### Personal Expenses
**Prohibited Items**
- Personal meals, entertainment, and shopping
- Spouse or family travel and expenses
- Personal vehicle maintenance and repairs
- Personal insurance and medical expenses
- Personal communication and internet charges
- Alcoholic beverages outside business meals
- Personal gifts and charitable donations
- Fines, penalties, and traffic violations

### Policy Violations
**Excessive or Unreasonable Expenses**
- First-class travel without approval
- Luxury accommodations beyond business need
- Excessive meal and entertainment costs
- Personal use of company resources
- Unapproved or unnecessary expenses

## Compliance and Monitoring

### Audit and Review
**Expense Auditing**
- Random audit of expense reports
- Detailed review of high-dollar expenses
- Pattern analysis for policy compliance
- Corporate card statement reconciliation
- Annual policy compliance assessment

**Violation Consequences**
- Reimbursement denial for non-compliant expenses
- Required repayment of inappropriate reimbursements
- Corporate card privileges suspension or revocation
- Progressive disciplinary action
- Potential termination for serious violations

### Record Retention
**Documentation Requirements**
- Expense reports retained for 7 years
- Supporting receipts and documentation
- Approval and authorization records
- Corporate card statements and reconciliations
- Audit trails and compliance documentation
