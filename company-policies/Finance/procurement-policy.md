# Procurement Policy

## Purpose and Objectives

This policy establishes standardized procedures for purchasing goods and services to ensure cost-effective procurement, vendor management, and compliance with legal and regulatory requirements while maintaining quality and supporting business objectives.

## Procurement Principles

### Core Values
- **Value for Money**: Achieve best total cost of ownership
- **Transparency**: Open and fair procurement processes
- **Integrity**: Ethical conduct in all procurement activities
- **Accountability**: Clear responsibility and decision-making authority
- **Sustainability**: Environmental and social responsibility considerations
- **Risk Management**: Identify and mitigate procurement risks

### Strategic Objectives
- Support business goals and operational requirements
- Optimize total cost of ownership across product lifecycle
- Develop strategic supplier relationships and partnerships
- Ensure supply chain resilience and continuity
- Promote innovation and continuous improvement
- Maintain compliance with legal and regulatory requirements

## Procurement Authority and Approval Limits

### Approval Hierarchy
**Department Managers**
- Up to $5,000 per transaction
- Routine operational purchases
- Pre-approved vendor transactions
- Budget line item purchases
- Emergency purchases under $2,500

**Directors**
- $5,001 to $25,000 per transaction
- Capital equipment purchases
- New vendor approvals under $25,000
- Service contracts and agreements
- Multi-year commitments under $50,000

**Vice Presidents**
- $25,001 to $100,000 per transaction
- Strategic vendor partnerships
- Major capital investments
- Enterprise software licenses
- Significant service agreements

**Executive Leadership**
- Over $100,000 per transaction
- Strategic acquisitions and investments
- Major infrastructure projects
- Enterprise-wide system implementations
- Long-term strategic partnerships

### Procurement Methods by Value
**Small Purchases ($0 - $5,000)**
- Direct purchase from approved vendors
- Petty cash for immediate needs under $100
- Corporate credit card purchases
- Simplified approval process
- Basic documentation requirements

**Medium Purchases ($5,001 - $50,000)**
- Competitive quotes from 3+ vendors
- Formal purchase order required
- Vendor evaluation and selection
- Contract terms negotiation
- Comprehensive documentation

**Large Purchases ($50,001 - $250,000)**
- Formal competitive bidding process
- Request for Proposal (RFP) or Request for Quote (RFQ)
- Cross-functional evaluation team
- Detailed vendor assessment
- Executive approval required

**Major Purchases (Over $250,000)**
- Comprehensive procurement strategy
- Market analysis and benchmarking
- Formal tender or bidding process
- Board approval for strategic purchases
- Legal review and contract negotiation
